---

## 4. One-Sentence Description

A proposal to apply time-based cost mechanisms to long-term tax deferral within a realization-based system, preserving realization while correcting permanent deferral distortions.

---

## 5. Core Framing Principle

The framework does not replace realization-based taxation.

It introduces time as an economically meaningful variable where prolonged deferral has previously been costless.

The goal is structural correction, not confiscation.

---

## 6. What This Is Not

- Not a wealth tax
- Not forced realization
- Not asset confiscation
- Not anti-wealth
- Not a political campaign
- Not an ideological movement

It is a structural refinement of an existing system.

---

## 8. Core Concept (Plain English)

The income tax system taxes gains when realized.

At extreme scale and over long durations, realization can become optional.

When time carries no cost, indefinite deferral becomes structurally dominant.

This framework restores balance by ensuring that prolonged deferral carries a time-based cost — without requiring asset sales or annual wealth taxation.

---

## 9. Short Elevator Version (15 Seconds)

“Time Isn’t Free” is about making long-term tax deferral carry a time cost instead of forcing realization. It preserves the realization system but corrects what happens when deferral becomes permanent.
